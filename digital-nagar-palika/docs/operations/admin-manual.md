# Municipal operator guide

1. Sign in through the municipality-approved account and MFA/OTP process. Use only your assigned organization, ward and department scope.
2. Check the dashboard for assigned work and pending approvals. Blank figures mean the service/API is not connected; do not treat blanks as zero.
3. Verify a household using the municipality-approved evidence process. Record only necessary fields and keep correction reason and source.
4. Triage complaints, assign a responsible department/officer, and record each status change. Ask for missing information through approved channels. Record field actions and attach only relevant evidence.
5. Acknowledge, review and publish public notices using the configured approval workflow. Confirm dates, Marathi/Hindi/English copy, source and audience before release.
6. Download exports only for an approved operational purpose and store them in authorized systems. Report suspicious access or data disclosure immediately.

Never share credentials, export full citizen lists without documented authority, publish unverified emergency numbers, or ask AI to make a binding decision. Role, contact and service configuration are restricted to approved administrators.
