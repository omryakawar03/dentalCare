# नागरिक मार्गदर्शक / नागरिक मार्गदर्शिका / Citizen guide

Choose मराठी, हिन्दी or English at any time. Register with your mobile number and complete the municipality's verification steps. Scanning a ward or municipality QR only chooses a service area; it does not reveal private household or citizen information. Do not share OTPs.

To submit a grievance, choose a category, explain the issue, confirm the location and attach a relevant image if useful. Save the complaint reference. You can review status updates and request reopening when the issue remains unresolved. Applications that require an in-person visit or official system will be labelled **Request / Application** rather than instant approval.

Read public notices and schemes with their official source links. Emergency contact details are displayed only after official verification. Never rely on an AI answer as an official eligibility or legal decision; contact the municipal office when information is missing. Use the help/contact section for accessibility or account assistance.
